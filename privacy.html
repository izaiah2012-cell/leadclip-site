Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 November 2026

LeadClip ("LeadClip", "we", "us") is a Chrome extension that saves the LinkedIn profile you are viewing to your own Google Sheet or Notion database. This policy explains what information we handle, why, and your choices. LeadClip is operated by Edward Kennedy, trading as LeadClip, a sole trader based in the United Kingdom.

1. The short version

  • Profiles you clip go directly from your browser to your own Google Sheet or Notion database. We do not keep a copy of your leads.
  • We store only what we need to run your account: your Google account ID and email, your plan, and how many clips you've used this month.
  • We never sell your data, and we don't use it for advertising.

3. When LeadClip reads web pages

LeadClip only reads a LinkedIn profile page when you open the extension on that page and click Clip Lead. It does not run in the background, track your browsing or read other websites.

4. Google user data

If you connect Google Sheets, LeadClip asks for permission to create and edit only the spreadsheets that LeadClip itself creates (the drive.file scope). LeadClip cannot see or change your other files.

LeadClip's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. We use Google user data only to provide LeadClip's features, we do not transfer it to others except as needed to provide those features or as required by law, we do not use it for advertising, and no human reads it unless you ask us to for support.

5. Notion data

If you connect Notion, you choose which pages LeadClip can access on Notion's own approval screen. LeadClip uses this access only to create a leads database and add the profiles you clip. You can remove LeadClip's access at any time in Notion under Settings → Connections.

6. Service providers

We use a small number of trusted providers to run LeadClip: Vercel (hosting), a database provider for account records, and Stripe (payment processing). They process data only on our instructions.

7. How long we keep data

We keep your account record while your account is active. If you ask us to delete your account, we delete your record within 30 days, except where we must keep payment records for legal or tax reasons.

8. Your rights

If you are in the UK or EU, you have the right to access, correct, delete or export your personal data, and to object to or restrict how we use it. Contact us at the address below and we'll respond within one month. You can also complain to the UK Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk).

9. Removing LeadClip

  • Uninstall the extension from Chrome to remove all data stored on your device.
  • Revoke Google access at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • Revoke Notion access in Notion under Settings → Connections.
  • Email us to delete your LeadClip account record.

10. Children

LeadClip is a business tool and is not intended for anyone under 18.

11. Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update the date above and, for significant changes, let you know in the extension.
