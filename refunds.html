Refund Policy

Last updated: 17 November 2026

We want you to be happy with LeadClip. This policy explains how refunds work for LeadClip Pro and the Founding member plan.

1. 14-day money-back guarantee

If you're not satisfied, you can request a full refund within 14 days of your purchase, for any reason. This applies to:

  • Pro Monthly (£6/month): your first payment
  • Pro Yearly (£48/year): your first payment, and any yearly renewal if you ask within 14 days of being charged
  • Founding member (£39 one-off): your payment

2. How to request a refund

Our order process is conducted by our online reseller Paddle.com, the Merchant of Record for all our orders. You can request a refund in either of these ways:

  • Reply to your Paddle receipt email, or visit paddle.net to find your order and request a refund.
  • Email us at leadclipsupport@gmail.com with the email address you paid with, and we'll arrange it.

Refunds go back to your original payment method, usually within 5–10 working days depending on your bank.

3. Cancelling a subscription

You can cancel Pro at any time from LeadClip Settings → Manage subscription. Cancelling stops future renewals, and you keep Pro until the end of the period you've paid for. Cancelling on its own doesn't refund the current period. Use the 14-day guarantee above if you want your money back.

4. After 14 days

After 14 days, payments are generally non-refundable, but if something has gone wrong (for example, you were charged twice or LeadClip stopped working for you), please contact us and we'll look at it fairly.

5. Your statutory rights

This policy doesn't affect your legal rights as a consumer under UK law.
