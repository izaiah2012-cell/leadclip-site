Safe by design

Last updated: 26 November 2026

A lot of LinkedIn tools work by scraping in bulk or automating clicks in the background, which is what gets accounts restricted. LeadClip is built the other way round. Here is exactly what it does, and what it doesn't.

1. One click, one profile

  • LeadClip only reads the single profile you are looking at, and only when you click Clip Lead.
  • It does not run in the background, open pages for you, scroll search results, send connection requests or messages, or visit LinkedIn on its own.
  • There is no bulk mode and no automation. Saving 20 profiles means you opened 20 profiles yourself.

2. What it saves, and where

  • It saves what is visible on the profile you're viewing: name, headline, current company, location and the profile link, plus the date.
  • On Pro, it can also save an email address, phone number or website, only if you have opened that person's Contact info panel and LinkedIn is showing it to you. LeadClip never looks up, guesses or buys contact details.
  • Those details go straight from your browser to your own Google Sheet or Notion database. They are never sent to or stored on LeadClip's servers.
  • LeadClip's server only holds what it needs to run your account: your email, your plan and your monthly clip count. See the Privacy Policy.

4. LinkedIn's terms

LeadClip is an independent product and is not affiliated with or endorsed by LinkedIn. LinkedIn's User Agreement does not allow scraping or automated access to its site, which is why LeadClip has no automation: it only acts when you click, on a page you have opened yourself.

We can't speak for LinkedIn or guarantee how it treats any browser extension, so please use LeadClip in a normal, human way and use the details you save responsibly. You are responsible for how you store and use the information, including under data protection laws such as UK and EU GDPR. Don't use LeadClip to build lists for sale or to send spam.

5. You stay in control

  • Uninstall the extension to remove everything stored on your computer.
  • Revoke Google access at myaccount.google.com/permissions, or Notion access under Settings → Connections.
  • Email leadclipsupport@gmail.com to delete your LeadClip account record.
