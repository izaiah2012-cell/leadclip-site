Questions

Do I need any technical setup?

No. Sign in with Google and LeadClip creates your Google Sheet automatically, or connect Notion and LeadClip creates the database with the right columns.

What does LeadClip save?

The details shown on the profile you're viewing: full name, first and last name, headline, current company, location and profile URL, plus the date you clipped it. You choose which columns to fill in Settings.

Does LeadClip store my leads?

No. Clipped profiles go directly from your browser to your own Google Sheet or Notion database. See our Privacy Policy.

Does it scrape LinkedIn in bulk?

No. LeadClip only reads the single profile you are viewing, when you click Clip Lead. It never runs in the background, sends messages or opens pages for you. How LeadClip stays safe.

Can LeadClip find email addresses or phone numbers?

No. LeadClip doesn't look up or guess contact details. Tools that do pull data from other databases, which costs more and adds risk. LeadClip saves what's on the profile you're viewing, straight to your own sheet. On Pro, if you open a profile's Contact info and LinkedIn shows you an email, phone or website, LeadClip can save that too.

Why are there Full name, First name and Last name columns?

First name lets you personalise messages ("Hi Sarah"), and separate first and last names import cleanly into CRMs like HubSpot, Salesforce and Dynamics 365, which need them split. Full name is there for easy reading. You can hide any column you don't need in your sheet.

What happens after my 15 free clips?

Your free clips reset every month. If you need more, upgrade to Pro for unlimited clips. Cancel any time.
